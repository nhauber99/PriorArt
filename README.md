# PriorArt — Defensive Publications

This repository contains technical disclosures for projects, prototypes, and research ideas that I am making publicly available for defensive-publication purposes.

The goal is to create dated, publicly accessible prior-art records so that the disclosed ideas, embodiments, variations, and combinations are available to the public and may help prevent later patenting of the same or obvious technical subject matter by others.

These documents are not patent applications, legal opinions, product documentation, or guarantees of freedom to operate. Implementing any disclosed idea may still require checking existing patents, licenses, regulations, and third-party rights.

## Disclosures

### TextureScanner / Device for Digitizing Materials

A defensive publication describing material-scanning devices and methods, including photometric stereo, SVBRDF/BRDF capture, polarized reflection and transmission capture, spectral illumination, material-map generation, renderable material output, calibration, machine-learning-assisted reconstruction, quality control, and related embodiments.

The current expanded version explicitly includes multiple embodiments and subcombinations, including front-side reflection capture combined with polarized back-side transmission capture, direct-transmission and depolarized-translucency map generation, spectral and polarization variants, OpenPBR/MaterialX-style output, and related processing methods.
